UNIVERSAL COLLECTIBLE OBJECTS — v1

All detective cases use src/collectibles.js.
Case files contain DATA only: image, x/y/w/h percentages, evidence id, requirements.
The engine renders inside the same .scene-stage as the location background.
Therefore background and objects scale/crop together on every screen size.
Collected objects are not rendered again after state.evidence is saved.
Hover/focus reveals the label on mouse/keyboard; touch directly collects the visible object.
Coarse-pointer devices receive a larger invisible hit area without resizing the PNG.

- Family Recipe: universal layer and renderer are connected; collectibleDefs is ready for object assets.
